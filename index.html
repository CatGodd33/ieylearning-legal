Español / English

ieylerning puts a shield over the apps that eat your time. Complete your chosen challenge in ieylerning to unlock them. Each unlock has its own timer of at least 15 minutes. A system monitor brings the shield back when the time ends; iOS may delay its callback, so you can also reblock earlier from Home. You can also study without enabling protection.

ieylerning is free. There are no purchases, no subscriptions and no ads, and every word pack is included.

Your library stays on your iPhone. Signing in with Apple is optional, and the app can be used without an account.
